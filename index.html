The Bakehouse Creed

No Shortcuts. No Improvers. Just Flour, Water, Salt & Time.

At Breadly, we wake up at 3:30 AM while our neighborhood is still asleep. What began in 2019 as Meera and Arjun’s quest for authentic crusty bread has grown into a neighborhood hearth where real bread is honored as craft.

We mill stone-ground heritage Indian grains alongside imported French flours, slowly developing flavor through natural fermentation that is gentle on your digestion and bursting with complex wheaty depth.

🌾

Organic Heritage Grains

Locally sourced, stone-milled Khapli (Emmer) wheat rich in nutrients with low glycemic impact.

🧈

84% Normandy Butter Lamination

Twenty-seven delicate layers rolled by hand for that signature buttery honeycomb shatter.

🔥

Stone Hearth Deck Baking

Baked straight on high-heat stone decks with steam injection for a blistered, caramelized crust.
