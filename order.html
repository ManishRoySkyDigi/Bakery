Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to book? +
We require at least 48 hours for standard bespoke cakes and 1 week for multi-tiered wedding cakes or catering orders. For weekend dates, we recommend booking 4–5 days prior as our oven slots fill quickly.
Do you offer 100% eggless options? +
Yes! Our Valrhona Dark Truffle, Spiced Carrot Pecan, and Mango Passionfruit cakes can all be crafted 100% eggless using organic condensed milk and natural fruit pectin binders with no compromise on texture.
How does delivery work in Metro District? +
We offer direct bakehouse pickup at our neighborhood 100ft Road, or temperature-controlled four-wheeler car delivery via our dedicated delivery partners across our neighborhood, Koramangala, CBD, Whitefield, and HSR Layout.
What are your payment terms for custom bakes? +
A 50% advance via UPI (GPay / PhonePe / Paytm) confirms your date on our bake calendar. The remaining balance is payable upon delivery or pickup.
