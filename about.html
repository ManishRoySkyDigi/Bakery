Our Origins

It Started With A Single Dutch Oven.

In late 2018, Meera and Arjun were software architects who spent their weekends obsessed with the science of wild fermentation. After dozens of dense loaves, burnt bottoms, and sticky kitchen floors, they successfully birthed 'Bob'—the wild sourdough starter that powers every single loaf at Breadly today.

They began handing out warm crusty loaves to neighbors on our neighborhood’s 12th Main. Within six months, Sunday morning lines began forming outside their apartment door. In winter 2019, they left their corporate desks, imported a two-deck French stone oven, and opened Breadly.

Today, we still weigh every ingredient by hand, fold each batch of dough with care, and refuse all artificial dough conditioners, bromates, or fast commercial yeasts.

Philosophy

What We Stand For Every Morning

🌾

Soil & Heritage First

We partner directly with organic heritage wheat farmers. We pay above-market fair trade rates for unbleached, nutrient-dense grain.

⏳

The Discipline of Time

Industrial bread takes 2 hours with chemical accelerants. Our bread takes 48 hours. This slow breakdown of complex starches ensures easy digestion and unparalleled aroma.

🤝

Community Fellowship

We donate our day-end unsliced loaves to local community kitchens every evening. A bakery should nourish the entire neighborhood, without exception.
